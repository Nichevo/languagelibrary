Chinese Podcast Episode #7 (Simplified Characters)

Dialogue #36
王：这是什么？
李：这是我家人的照片。
王：一、二、三、四、五、六，你家有六个人吗？
李：我家有五个人。这个是我姐姐的男朋友。
王：Mmmm, 你姐姐的男朋友很高、很白，他是哪国人？
李：他是英国人。

Dialogue #37
李：你有姐妹吗？
王：有，我有两个姐姐、（or 和）一个妹妹。你呢？
李：我也有两个姐姐，没有妹妹。
王：你有你姐姐的照片吗？
李：有。…. 这是我大姐，这是我二姐。
王：什么？这是你大姐？她是我哥哥的女朋友。

Dialogue #38
王：你爸妈做什么工作？
李：我爸爸是医生，（我）妈妈没有工作。
王：是吗？我爸爸也是医生。
李：你妈妈有工作吗？
王：有。她是北京大学的英文老师。

Dialogue #39
王：请问，你是这个大学的老师吗？
李：不是，我是学生。你呢？
王：我也是学生。你叫什么名字？
李：我叫李友生。

Dialogue #40
王：你有哥哥、弟弟吗？
李：我有两个哥哥，没有弟弟。
王：你哥哥都是大学生吗？
李：我哥哥都是律师。
王：是吗？我有两个弟弟，他们也都是律师。

Dialogue #41
王：你是哪个大学的学生？
李：我是德州大学的学生。
王：是吗？我也是德州大学的学生。你学什么？
李：我学中文。你呢？
王：我也学中文。你的中文老师姓什么？
李：我们有两个老师，一个姓高，一个姓谢。
王：他们都是中国人吗？
李：高老师是美国人，他的中文很不好。

Chinese Podcast Episode #7 (English Translations)

Dialogue #36
Wang: What is this?
Li:
This is a picture of my family.
Wang: (Counting) one, two, three, four, five, six. Does your family have six
people?
Li:
There are five people in my family.
(Pointing at a man in the picture) This one is my older sister’s boyfriend.
Wang: Mmmm, your older sister’s boyfriend is tall and fair. Where is he from?
Li:
He is from U.K.

Dialogue #37
Li:
Do you have sisters?
Wang: Yes, I have two older sisters and one younger sister. How about you?
Li:
I also have two older sisters; I don’t have any younger sisters.
Wang: Do you have your sisters’ pictures?
Li:
Yes. (Taking out a couple of pictures) This is my eldest sister, and this is
my second eldest sister.
Wang: What? This is your eldest sister? She is my older brother’s girlfriend.

Dialogue #38
Wang: What (line of) work do your parents do?
Li:
My father is a doctor. My mother does not have a job.
Wang: Is that so? My father is also a doctor.
Li:
Does your mother have a job?
Wang: Yes, she does. She is an English teacher at Beijing University.

Dialogue #39
Wang: Excuse me. Are you a teacher of this university?
Li:
No, I am a student. How about you?
Wang: I am also a student. What is your name?
Li:
My name is Yousheng Li.

Dialogue #40
Wang: Do you have brothers?
Li:
I have two older brothers, and no younger brothers.
Wang: Are your brothers both college students?
Li:
My brothers are both lawyers.
Wang: Is that so? I have two younger brothers. Both of them are also lawyers.

Dialogue #41
(Two college students are having a conversation.)
Wang: What school do you go to?
Li:
I go to the University of Texas (UT).
Wang: Is that so? I also go to UT. What do you study?
Li:
I study Chinese. How about you?
Wang: I study Chinese, too. What is your Chinese teacher’s last name?
Li:
We have two teachers. One’s last name is Gao; one’s last name is Xie.
Wang: Are they both Chinese?
Li:
Teacher Gao is an American. His Chinese is very bad.
