Chinese Podcast Episode #4 (English Translations)

Dialogue #22
(A secretary greets someone on the job for the first day.)
Secretary: Hello! What is your last name?
New employee: My last name is Wang.
Secretary: Mr. Wang, my last name is Li.
New employee: Hello, Miss. Li.

Dialogue #23
(A teacher greets two students.)
Teacher (to the girl): Hello! What is your name?
Student A: My name is Xiaopeng Li.
Teacher (to the boy): How about you?
Student B: My last name is also Li. My (full) name is Gui Li.

Dialogue #24
(A new student approaches the registration desk. A man is sitting behind the desk.)
Man: Hello. What is your last name?
Student: My last name is Wang.
Man: (Checking the roster….) Is your name Mingyou Wang?
Student: My name is not Mingyou Wang; my name is Guipeng Wang.
Man: O…. (He found his name.) OK!
Student: What is your last name?
Man: My last name is also Wang.
Student: How are you, Mr. Wang?
Man: I am your teacher. Please call me Teacher Wang.

Dialogue #25
(At a party)
Male: Hello, what is your last name?
Female: My last name is Li.
Male: Miss Li, my last name is Wang.
Female: Hello, Mr. Wang.
Male: My name is Wang Gui. All of my friends call me Little Wang.
Female: I will also call you Little Wang, OK?
Male: OK! What do I call you? Shall I call you Miss Li?
Female: My name is Youyou Li. Please call me Youyou.

Dialogue #26
(Office worker, Mr. Wang, observes that his co-worker, Mr. Li, is reading a novel
at his desk.)
Wang: Are you not busy, Mr. Li?
Li: I am not. How about you?
Wang: I am busy.
