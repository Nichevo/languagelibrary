Chinese Podcast Episode #6 (Simplified Characters)

Dialogue #31
王: 这是什么？
李：这是我男朋友的照片。
王：你男朋友是美国人吗？
李：他爸爸是美国人，他妈妈是中国人。
王：他有中文名字吗？
李：他爸爸没有中文名字，他妈妈姓高，他的中文名字叫高友生。

Dialogue #32
李：这是谁？
王：这是我姐姐。
李：这也是你姐姐吗？
王：不是，那是我姐姐的女儿。
李：你姐姐有儿子吗？
王：她有一个女儿、一个儿子。

Dialogue #33
王：这个人是谁？
李：那是我爸爸。
王：这个呢？
李：那是我哥哥。
王：这是你妈妈吗？
李：不是，那是我爸爸的姐姐。

Dialogue #34
王：这五个人是谁？
李：这是我爸爸，这是我妈妈，这三个是我哥哥。
王：这是你大哥吗？
李：不是，那是我二哥。

Dialogue #35
老师：你叫什么名字？
学生：我叫 Vincent Lee。
老师：你没有中文名字吗？
学生：有，我有两个中文名字，一个叫李文生，一个叫文勝利。
老师：啊，这两个名字都很好。

学生：谢谢！请问你贵姓？
老师：我姓王，我是你的老师，请叫我王老师。

Chinese Podcast Episode #6 (English Translations)

Dialogue #31
Wang: What is this?
Li:
This is a picture of my boyfriend.
Wang: Is your boyfriend an American?
Li:
His father is an American; his mother is Chinese.
Wang: Does he have a Chinese name?
Li:
His father does not have a Chinese name; his mother’s last name is Gao.
His Chinese name is Yousheng Gao.

Dialogue #32
(Wang is showing a couple of photographs to Li.)
Li:
Who is this?
Wang: This is my older sister.
Li:
(Point at another photo) Is this also your older sister?
Wang: No, that is my older sister’s daughter.
Li:
Does your older sister have sons?
Wang: She has one daughter and one son.

Dialogue #33
(Wang is looking at Li’s family picture.)
Wang: Who is this man?
Li:
That is my father.
Wang: How about this one?
Li:
That is my older brother.
Wang: Is this your mother?
Li:
No, that is my father’s older sister.

Dialogue #34
(Wang is looking at a picture in a frame on Li’s desk.)
Wang: Who are these five people?
Li:
This is my father; this is my mother; these three are my older brothers.
Wang: Is this your eldest brother?
Li:
No, that is my second eldest brother.

Dialogue #35
Teacher: What is your name?
Student: My name is Vincent Lee.
Teacher: Don’t you have a Chinese name?
Student: Yes. I have two Chinese names. One is Li Wensheng; one is Wen
Shengli.
Teacher: Ah! Both of these names are good.
Student: Thanks! May I ask your last name please?
Teacher: My last name is Wang. I am your teacher. Please call me Teacher Wang.
