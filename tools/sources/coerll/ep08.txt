李：没有。我这几天都不忙。

的学生；可是二〇〇八年她是纽约大学的学生。

Dialogue #44
Male: When (=Which day) is your birthday?
Female: July 16.
Male: July 16! Today is Monday, July 13. Then your birthday is this Thursday.
I am free that evening. How about if I take you out to dinner?
Female: Thank you! But I will be busy that evening because I have a big test on
Friday.
Male: How old are you this year?
Female: I will turn 20 this year.
Male: Ah! 20th birthday is a big birthday. You are alone in New York, and
none of your family members are here. …..Mmmm, I am also free Friday
evening. How about if I treat you Friday evening?
Female: Ah! Thank you. You are extremely nice.
Male: You are welcome. What time shall we meet?
Female: How about 7:15?
Male: OK. See you at 7:15 on Friday.
Female: Goodbye!
Male: Goodbye.
