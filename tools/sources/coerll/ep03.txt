Chinese Podcast Episode #3 (Simplified Characters)
Dialogue #14
A: 这是谁的车？是你的吗？
B: 不是，那是我爸爸的车，我没有车。
A: 你爸爸的车是美国车吗？
B: 是，我妈妈的车也是美国车。你爸妈有车吗？
A: 有，他们的车也都是国车。

Dialogue #15
A: 你是学生吗？
B: 是，我是学生， 你也是学生吗？
A: 不是，我是老师。我姓王。
B: 王老师，你好。
A: 你好。你学什么？
B: 我学中文。
A: 是吗？你的中文老师姓什么？
B: 他姓谢。
A: Ah! 谢老师是我的好朋友。

Dialogue #16
A: 你家有几辆车？
B: 两辆，我爸爸有一辆，我也有一辆。
A: 你妈妈没有车吗？
B: 没有，我弟弟也没有车。
A: 你的车是哪国车？
B: 我的车是日本车，我爸爸的是美国车。

Dialogue #17
A: 这个人是谁？
B: 是我哥哥。
A: Ah! 你哥哥很高。这个是谁？
B: 那也是我哥哥。
A: 是吗？你有几个哥哥？
B: 两个；一个是医生，一个是律师。
A: 哪个是医生？
B: 这个，他是我大哥，这个是二哥。

Dialogue #18
A: 高先生，你有几个小孩？
B: 四个；我有三个女儿，还有一个儿子。
A: 你儿子叫什么名字？
B: 他叫高大中。
A: 他做什么工作？
B: 他还是个大学生，没有工作。

Dialogue #19
A: 这儿有两辆车，哪辆是你的？
B: 那两辆车都不是我的，我的车是日本车。
A: 那你的车在哪儿？
B: 在那儿！那儿有一辆日本车，那是我的车。

Dialogue #20
A: 白小姐，你有男朋友吗？
B: 有。
A: 你有他的照片吗？
B: 有，这儿有七、八张他的照片。
…… 这是我男朋友，这几个是他家人。
A: 你男朋友是外国人吗？

B: 他是美国人，他家在纽约。
A: 他有中文名字吗？
B: 有，他的中文名字叫张文生。

Dialogue #21
A: 你家在哪儿？
B: 我家有六个人，我爸妈在外国，我哥哥、姐姐都在北京。
A: 你哥哥、姐姐做什么工作？
B: 我的两个哥哥都是北京大学的学生；我姐姐是那儿的老师。

Chinese Podcast Episode #3 (English Translations)
Dialogue #14
A: Whose car is this? Is it yours?
B: No, that is my father’s car. I don’t have a car.
A: Is your father’s car an American car?
B: Yes. My mother’s car is also an American car. Do your parents have cars?
A: Yes. Both of their cars are also American cars.

Dialogue #15
A: Are you a student?
B: Yes, I am a student. Are you also a student?
A: No. I am a teacher. My last name is Wang.
B: Hello, Teacher Wang.
A: Hello. What do you study?
B: I study Chinese.
A: Is that so? What is your Chinese teacher’s last name?
B: His last name is Xie.
A: Ah! Teacher Xie is my good friend.

Dialogue #16
A: How many cars does your family have?
B: Two. My father has one; I have one.
A: Does your mother not have a car?
B: No. My younger brother does not have a car, either.
A: What kind (country of origin) of car your car?
B: My car is a Japanese car. My father’s is an American car.

Dialogue #17
A: Who is this person?
B: It’s my older brother.
A: Ah! Your brother is tall. Who is this one?
B: That is also my older brother.
A: Is that so? How many older brothers do you have?
B: Two; one is a doctor; one is a lawyer.
A: Which one is a doctor?
B: This one; he is my eldest brother. This one is my second eldest brother.

Dialogue #18
A: How many children do you have, Mr. Gao?
B: Four. I have three daughters; I also have one son.
A: What is your son’s name?
B: His name is Dazhong Gao.
A: What (kind of) work does he do?
B: He is still a college (university) student; he does not have a job.

Dialogue #19
A: There are two cars here. Which one is yours?
B: Neither of those two cars are mine. My car is a Japanese car.
A: Then where is your car?
B: It’s over there! There is a Japanese car over there. That is my car.

Dialogue #20
A: Do you have a boyfriend, Miss Bai?
B: Yes.
A: Do you have his pictures?
B: Yes. There are seven or eight pictures of his here. …… This is my boyfriend;
these few are his family (members).
A: Is your boyfriend a foreigner?
B: He is an American. His home is in New York.

A: Does he have a Chinese name?
B: Yes. His Chinese name is Wensheng Zhang.

Dialogue #21
A: Where is your home?
B: My family has six people. My parents are abroad (in a foreign country). My
older brothers and older sister are in Beijing.
A: What do your older brothers and older sister do?
B: Both of my older brothers are students at Beijing University. My older sister is
a teacher there.
