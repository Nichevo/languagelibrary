Chinese Podcast Episode #2 (Simplified Characters)
Dialogue #8
A: 你有姐妹吗？有几个？
B: 我没有姐姐，有一个妹妹。你呢？
A: 我有一个姐姐，没有妹妹。你有哥哥、弟弟吗？
B: 没有。你呢？
A: 我也没有哥哥，我有一个弟弟。

Dialogue #9
A: 你贵姓？
B: 我姓王，叫王贵一。
A: 王小姐，你好。
B: 请叫我贵一，好吗？你贵姓？
A: 我姓李。

Dialogue #10
A: 你哥哥有几个小孩？
B: 我大哥有一个女儿，二哥还没有小孩。你哥哥有小孩吗?
A: 我有一个姐姐，没有哥哥。她有一个男孩。

Dialogue #11
A: 你有男朋友吗？
B: 还没有。你呢？
A: 我也还没有。
Dialogue #12
A: 这是谁？是你女朋友吗？
B: 不是，她是我姐姐。
A: 你有你女朋友的照片吗？
B: 有一张。…… 这是我，这是我女朋友。
A: 这个呢？
B: 我女朋友是老师，那个女孩是她的学生。
Dialogue #13
A: 这是谁的照片？是你女朋友的吗？
B: 不是，这是王先生的女朋友。
A: 王先生？王先生是谁？
B: 他是我哥哥的老师，他女朋友也是我哥哥的老师。

Chinese Podcast Episode #2 (English Translations)
Dialogue #8
A: Do you have sisters? How many do you have?
B: I do not have older sisters; I have one younger sister. How about you?
A: I have one older sister; I don’t have younger sisters. Do you have brothers?
B: No. How about you?
A: I don’t have older brothers, either. I have one younger brother.

Dialogue #9
A: What is your honorable last name?
B: My last name is Wang; my name is Guiyi Wang.
A: Hello, Miss Wang.
B: Please call me Guiyi, OK? What is your honorable last name?
A: My last name is Li.

Dialogue #10
A: How many children do your brothers have?
B: My eldest brother has a daughter; my second eldest brother does not have
children yet. Does your older brother have children?
A: I have an older sister; I don’t have old brothers. She has a boy.

Dialogue #11
A: Do you have a boyfriend?
B: Not yet. How about you?
A: I don’t have one yet, either.

Dialogue #12
A: Who is this? Is it your girlfriend?
B: No, she is my older sister.
A: Do you have your girlfriend’s pictures?
B: I have one…… This is me; this is my girlfriend.
A: What about this one?
B: My girlfriend is a teacher; that girl is her student.

Dialogue #13
A: Whose picture is this? Is it your girlfriend’s?
B: No, this is Mr. Wang’s girlfriend.
A: Mr. Wang? Who is Mr. Wang?
B: He is my older brother’s teacher. His girlfriend is also my older brother’s
teacher.
