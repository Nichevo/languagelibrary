Chinese Podcast Episode #5 (Simplified Characters)
Dialogue #27
A：请问，你姓李吗？
B：我不姓李，姓王。
A：王先生，你是老师吗？
B：我不是老师，是学生。你呢？
A：我是老师，我也姓王。请叫我王老师。

Dialogue #28
A：李名中，我问你，你是中国人吗？
B：我不是李名中，我也不是中国人。
A：对不起，你叫什么名字？
B：我叫王小京。
A：啊！你是王老师。你好吗？我也是老师，我姓李。
B：李老师，你好!

Dialogue #29
A：先生，请问你是中国人吗？
B：我不是中国人。
A：你的朋友呢?

B：她也不是中国人。
A：李友生是中国名字，你不是李友生吗？
B：我是美国人，我叫 Lee Johnson.

Dialogue #30
A：请问你是中国学生吗？
B：是，你也是中国学生吗？
A：我也是。我叫王国朋。
B：啊！我也姓王。我叫王贵。
A：我叫你小王，你叫我老王，好吗？
B：好。

Chinese Podcast Episode #5 (English Translations)

Dialogue #27
A: Excuse me (= May I please ask), is your last name Li?
B: My last name is not Li; it's Wang.
A: Mr. Wang, are you a teacher?
B: I am not a teacher; I am a student. How about you?
A: I am a teacher; my last name is also Wang. Please call me Teacher Wang.

Dialogue #28
(At a get-together where new and old teachers and students can get to know one
another)
A: Mingzhong Li, let me ask you, are you Chinese?
B: I am not Mingzhong Li; I am not Chinese, either.
A: Sorry. What is your name?
B: My name is Xiaojing Wang.
A: Ah! You are Teacher Wang. How are you? I am also a teacher. My last name
is Li.
B: How are you, Teacher Li?

Dialogue #29
(A man and a woman are about to enter the Chinese embassy to attend a reception.)
A: Sir, may I please ask if you are Chinese?
B: I am not Chinese.

A: What about your friend?
B: She is not Chinese, either.
(A checks B's invitation.)
A: Yousheng Li is a Chinese name. Aren't you Yousheng Li?
B: I am an American. My name is Lee Johnson.

Dialogue #30
A: Excuse me, are you a Chinese student?
B: Yes. Are you also a Chinese student?
A: Yes, I am, too. My name is Guopeng Wang.
B: Ah! My last name is also Wang. My name is Gui Wang.
A: I will call you Little Wang, (and) you call me Old Wang, OK?
B: OK.
